03 / Unit economics

Illustrative growth simulator

Adjust assumptions. This is a scenario tool, not a forecast or an approved budget. Defaults use the seven attributed funded deals for average ticket and revenue, but contribution margin remains an assumption requiring Finance validation.

Annualized scenario

Method: funded volume = monthly deals × average ticket; attributed revenue = funded volume × assumed revenue yield; contribution = attributed revenue × assumed contribution margin; net contribution = contribution − monthly growth operating costs. Revenue yield is based on observed attributed revenue / funded volume (~9.05%); it is not a forecast of future pricing. Operating costs are illustrative, and funding losses or other costs must be reflected in Finance's contribution definition.
04 / Execution and accountability

90-day controlled pilot

Days 1–15

Baseline + authority

  • Reconcile deal attribution and Finance revenue
  • Inventory dormant leads and stalled stages
  • Agree sponsor, capacity and cost cap
  • Publish shared KPI definitions

Gate: approved baseline and pilot charter

Days 16–45

Operate core engine

  • Launch managed outreach intake
  • Run one inbound/app recovery experiment
  • Establish routing and follow-up SLAs
  • Review campaign cost weekly

Gate: reliable execution and sales acceptance

Days 46–75

Optimize + expand

  • Improve conversion bottlenecks
  • Test one broker or field channel
  • Validate source attribution
  • Track lead cohorts and stage age

Gate: acceptable quality and capacity

Days 76–90

Investment decision

  • Reconcile actual pilot spend
  • Review mature funded cohorts
  • Assess incremental contribution
  • Recommend scale, revise or stop

Gate: CEO + Finance decision

05 / Management scorecard

What I will be accountable for

Weekly leading indicators

Eligible accounts reached · positive replies · sales-accepted opportunities · qualified meetings · revived applications · stage movement · response SLA · spend vs cap.

Monthly and mature-cohort outcomes

Funded deals · disbursement volume · attributed revenue · Finance-approved contribution · fully loaded cost per funded deal · time-to-fund by source · partner yield.

Every deal has one primary acquisition source. Recovery and other activation assists are recorded separately. Funding can take 50+ days, so immature cohorts cannot be treated as failures or successes prematurely. Targets and cost ceilings are signed off after the first 15-day baseline.
